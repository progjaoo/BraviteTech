Changes for Bravite:
- Surface maps converted to WebP for delivery.
- Neptune and Saturn retain 2048 x 1024 pixels.
- Moon optimized to 1024 x 512 pixels for its small on-screen diameter.
- Ring image retained at 2048 x 125 pixels.
- Cool material tints, lighting and atmospheric rims applied by Three.js.
- Moon albedo reused as a subtle artistic bump; this is not an elevation dataset.

These maps are artistic reconstructions based on astronomical imagery.
They are used as decorative imagery, not scientifically calibrated simulations.
